I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at [IIITDM Kancheepuram](https://www.iiitdm.ac.in/), Chennai.

My work sits at the point where machine learning meets hardware that was never designed to run it. Modern neural networks assume abundant memory and power; microcontrollers offer neither. I am interested in what has to change — in the model, the runtime, or the system architecture — to close that gap, and in what the resulting systems make possible in the field.

## Research Interests

* **Embedded machine learning (TinyML)** — deploying convolutional and time-series models on Cortex-M and Xtensa class devices under hard SRAM and flash constraints; quantization, layer tiling, and hand-written inference pipelines.
* **Distributed inference on microcontroller clusters** — partitioning networks across networked devices, and the fault tolerance that becomes possible once no single node holds the whole model.
* **Real-time and mixed-criticality systems** — scheduling guarantees for systems where tasks of differing criticality share a processor.
* **Applied reinforcement learning** — sequential decision-making for resource allocation, with recent attention to crop and greenhouse management.

Much of this work is collaborative across departments, including ongoing joint work with Mechanical Engineering on sensing for robotic manipulation.

## Teaching

I teach data structures and algorithms, and Python programming for engineering students. A recurring theme in how I approach both is that programs should be *watchable* — that students learn pointer manipulation and object identity far faster from a stepping visualizer than from a static diagram. The tools I build for this are freely available on the [Teaching](/teaching/) page and are open for other instructors to use.
