## 1. Interactive Code Visualizers
Use these custom-built tools to step through the logic of data structures and algorithms in real-time. **[▶ Open the Visualizer Hub](/files/visualizers/)**

The hub links to everything below, or you can jump straight to a tool:

* **[Binary Search Tree (BST)](/files/visualizers/Binary_Search_Tree.html)** – Insertion, deletion, and traversals (In-order, Pre-order, Post-order).
* **[AVL Tree](/files/visualizers/AVL_Tree.html)** – Watch the tree self-balance during insertions to prevent skewing.
* **[Expression Tree](/files/visualizers/Expression_Tree.html)** – Interactively build and evaluate expression trees.
* **[Heap](/files/visualizers/Heap.html)** – Heap operations and heapify, step by step.
* **[Hashing](/files/visualizers/Hashing.html)** – Hash-table insertion and collision handling.
* **[Quick Sort](/files/visualizers/Quick_Sort.html)** – Partitioning and the recursion tree, one step at a time.
* **[Binary Search](/files/visualizers/Binary_Search_Conceptual.html)** – The low / mid / high search on a sorted array, synced with the code.
* **[Growth of Functions](/files/visualizers/Growth_of_Functions.html)** – Compare asymptotic growth rates interactively.

*Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing to install, and no internet needed.*

> **Note to Students:** These visualizers are designed to supplement your lab sessions. Please ensure you have reviewed the primary lecture notes before attempting the quizzes.
